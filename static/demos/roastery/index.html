The brew ritual

Six minutes, start to first sip.

i.93°C

Wake the beans

Grind 18 g medium-fine, just before the water is ready. Never earlier — aroma is a currency you spend once.

ii.45 s

The bloom

Pour 40 g of water and watch it dome and sigh. This is the roast releasing a week of rest in one breath.

iii.2:30

Slow spirals

Pour in lazy circles to 300 g. If it looks meditative, you are doing it correctly.

iv.4:00

Draw down

A flat bed of grounds means an even cup. A crater means you got excited. Both are forgiven.

v.wait

Let it turn

Coffee opens as it cools, like wine the other direction. The third sip is the honest one.

vi.∞

Repeat tomorrow

Ritual beats recipe. Same chair, same cup, slightly better every single morning.
