The premise

Take weight out, and everything else gets easier.

01

Lightness

A lighter car needs less battery, less brake, less tyre and less structure. Every kilo removed removes another somewhere else.

02

Simplicity

Fewer systems, fewer failure points. The interface is a wheel, three pedals and a switch — the rest is deliberately absent.

03

Involvement

The point is not the lap time. It is the feeling of a small, honest car doing exactly what you asked of it.
