The Society

A club with
no clubhouse

It started with four people, one thoroughly average municipal course, and a shared suspicion that golf had got a little serious about itself.

We wanted clothes that could survive a wet November tee time and still look right at Sunday lunch — without anyone ever using the phrase “performance stretch technology” in our presence.

We are not fast. We have never been fast. We have, however, never once regretted the walk.
M. Ardleigh Founding member
& slowest of the four
01

Small batches, long memories

We cut two hundred of a thing and then we stop. If you missed it, you’ll survive — and something better is already on the table.

02

Play it as it lies

Weather, course conditions, your swing on the day. We make clothes for the round you actually get, not the one in the brochure.

03

Everyone plays through

No handicap requirement. No dress code enforcement. Nobody at the first tee quietly working out who you know.

04

The round doesn’t end at eighteen

Half of this is the walk in, the drink after, and the forty-minute argument about what happened on the fifth.

Giving Back

The Nineteenth Fund

Two per cent of every order goes to the courses without marketing budgets: municipal greens, community clubs, and junior memberships for kids whose families were never going to pay a joining fee.

No campaign, no ribbon, no landing page with a progress bar. It comes off the top, every order, and we publish where it went each January.
