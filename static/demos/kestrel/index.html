Three rules

We are allowed to say no to anything heavy.

i.

Subtract first

No component enters the car until we've failed to delete it twice. The lightest part is the one that isn't fitted.

ii.

One job each

The tub is the crash structure, the battery floor and the seat mount. Parts that do three jobs replace three parts.

iii.

Range is weight

A smaller battery in a lighter car goes as far as a big one in a heavy car — and it corners like something alive.
